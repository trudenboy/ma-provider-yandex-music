---
title: Recommendations
---

The provider generates **nine recommendation groups** on the Music Assistant home screen (Discover section). Each group updates independently on a different interval.

## Where They Appear

Recommendations appear on the **MA home screen** in the **Discover** section immediately after the provider is connected.

## Recommendation Groups

| Group | What it shows | Update interval |
|:-------|:---------------|:--------------------|
| **My Wave** | Personal playlist from Rotor | 10 minutes |
| **For You** | Algorithmic playlists (Déjà Vu, Premiere, Playlist of the Day, etc.) | 30 minutes |
| **Chart** | Yandex Music top tracks | 1 hour |
| **New Releases** | Fresh albums and singles | 1 hour |
| **New Playlists** | Recently added playlists | 1 hour |
| **Top Picks** | Best curated playlists | 1 hour |
| **Mood** | Randomly selected mood mix | 30 minutes |
| **Activity** | Randomly selected activity mix | 30 minutes |
| **Seasonal** | Current season playlist (auto-detected by month) | 6 hours |

## Notes

- **My Wave** and **For You** depend on your Yandex Music listening history.
- **Mood** and **Activity** randomly pick one of the available mixes each time — making the home screen a little different every visit.
- **Seasonal** automatically detects the current season by month and shows the corresponding playlist.
- If a group is empty (no data from the API) — it is not shown on the home screen.
